# Employee Offboarding Procedure (Sample)

*Fictional document for BrightCart. Supports Access Control Policy s5.3. Educational use only.*

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Procedure owner | HR Manager |
| Related policy | Access Control Policy v1.0 |
| Version | 2.0 (proposed in response to finding F-001) |

## Purpose
Remove a leaver's access to company systems within the policy timeframe and keep evidence.

## Steps
| # | Step | Responsible | Timing | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manager notifies HR of the leaving date | Line manager | At resignation or decision | HR record |
| 2 | HR creates a **termination ticket** with the leaving date | HR Manager | At least 2 business days before last day where possible | Ticket |
| 3 | Ticket automatically creates an IT task to disable accounts | IT Service Desk | On ticket creation | IT task |
| 4 | IT disables all accounts (email, e-commerce admin, cloud storage, VPN) and revokes MFA devices and tokens | IT Service Desk | Within 1 business day of termination | Disable log with timestamp |
| 5 | Second person verifies accounts are disabled | IT Manager or delegate | Within 1 business day | Verification note |
| 6 | Collect company equipment and update asset records | HR / IT | Last day | Asset record |
| 7 | Reconcile HR leaver list to active-account list | IT Manager | Weekly | Reconciliation record |
| 8 | Report exceptions to Head of Security | IT Manager | Weekly | Exception log |

## Exceptions
If access cannot be removed within one business day, the IT Manager records the reason and expected date, and the Head of Security is informed.

## Evidence retention
Tickets, disable logs and reconciliation records are kept for at least 2 years (illustrative; follow the organisation's retention rules).

## Review
Annually, or after any related finding.
