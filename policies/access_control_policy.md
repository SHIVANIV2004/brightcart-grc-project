# Access Control Policy (Sample)

| Version | 1.0 |

| Review frequency | Annually, or after significant change |

## 1. Purpose
To ensure access to BrightCart information and systems is authorised, appropriate to job responsibilities, and removed when no longer needed.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors and third parties who access BrightCart systems or information.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Policy owner (Head of Security) | Maintains the policy; reports on compliance |
| Control owners (IT Manager, HR Manager) | Operate account creation, changes and removal |
| Application owners | Approve access to their applications |
| Line managers | Request appropriate access; confirm leavers promptly |
| Internal reviewer (GRC) | Checks that reviews and evidence exist |

## 4. Policy statements
- **4.1** Every user must have an individual account. Shared accounts are not permitted except where an authorised exception exists.
- **4.2** Access is granted on a least-privilege basis, based on job responsibilities, and approved by the application owner.
- **4.3** Privileged (administrator) access requires separate approval and is limited to named individuals.
- **4.4** Multi-factor authentication is required for administrator accounts and remote access.

## 5. Joiners, movers and leavers
- **5.1** Access for new joiners is provided only after approval.
- **5.2** When a person changes role, access is reviewed and unneeded access removed.
- **5.3** Access for leavers must be removed within **1 business day** of termination. HR notifies IT through a tracked ticket.

## 6. Access review
- **6.1** Application owners review user access at least annually.
- **6.2** Privileged access is reviewed **quarterly**. The reviewer signs off, and each exception has a documented outcome.

## 7. Exceptions
Exceptions require written justification, approval by the policy owner, an expiry date, and are recorded in an exceptions log.

## 8. Compliance and enforcement
Failure to follow this policy may lead to disciplinary action. Compliance is checked through periodic reviews and audits.
