# Acceptable Use Policy (Sample)

*Fictional document for BrightCart. Educational use only.*

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Owner | Head of Security |
| Approver | CFO |
| Version | 1.0 |
| Review | Annually |

## 1. Purpose
To explain how staff may use BrightCart devices, accounts and information safely.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors and temporary staff using BrightCart systems or data, in the office or remotely.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **Staff:** follow this policy, complete security training, and report suspected incidents.
- **Line managers:** make sure their team knows and follows the policy.
- **IT and Security:** provide approved tools and monitor compliance.

## 4. Requirements
1. Use only approved applications and storage for company information.
2. Do not share passwords or use another person's account.
3. Use a unique password and MFA where required.
4. Lock your screen when away from your device.
5. Do not store customer or employee personal data on personal devices or personal messaging apps.
6. Be careful with email links and attachments; report suspected phishing.
7. Keep devices updated and report loss or theft immediately.
8. Do not install unapproved software.
9. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work, security or the company's reputation.

## 5. Reporting
Report suspected incidents to the Security team as soon as possible (timeframe defined in the Incident Reporting Procedure).

## 6. Exceptions
Require written approval from the Head of Security.

## 7. Enforcement
Breaches may lead to disciplinary action.

## 8. Review
Reviewed annually and after major changes.
