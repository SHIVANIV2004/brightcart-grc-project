# Management Summary: Information Security Assessment (Simulated)

*Fictional exercise for BrightCart. As of 2026-11-02.*

## Bottom line
The initial review found good basics in some areas (admin accounts, MFA, backup monitoring) but identified gaps in **evidence and follow-through**. Six findings need owner action; two are rated High.

## What needs a decision or attention
| Priority | Issue | What we know | What we need |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Leaver access not always removed within 1 business day | 3 of 10 sampled leavers late; one account was still active and has now been disabled | Approve ticket-based HR-to-IT process and weekly reconciliation by 2026-12-15 |
| High | Backup restores not tested in 2026 | Backups run daily, but recoverability is unverified | Infrastructure to complete and document a restore test by 2026-11-30 |
| Medium | Access review not signed off | 4 accounts flagged, no recorded outcome | Security to document outcomes by 2026-11-13 |
| Medium | Payroll vendor approved after contract start | Extra employee data is sent | HR to minimise data fields and update contract terms |
| Medium | Incident reporting timeframe undefined | Procedure says "promptly" | Approve a defined timeframe and out-of-hours contact |
| Low | Training completion 84% vs 95% target | 24 staff outstanding | Managers to follow up |

## Progress
- 10 actions agreed; 1 closed and validated; 1 overdue (training reminders, 3 days).
- No evidence reviewed shows that any account was misused. Activity logs for the late-disabled accounts have not yet been reviewed.

## Caveats
This is a limited, simulated review. Results should not be read as a full assessment of BrightCart's security or legal compliance.
